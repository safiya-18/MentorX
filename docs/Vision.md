# MentorX Vision

## Vision

MentorX is an AI-powered personal mentor designed to help GATE aspirants prepare with confidence through intelligent planning, personalized guidance, progress tracking, smart revision, and continuous motivation.

Instead of asking,

"What should I study today?"

MentorX already knows the answer.

---

## Mission

To remove the burden of planning so students can focus entirely on learning.

---

## Core Philosophy

MentorX is not just another study planner.

It behaves like a mentor who understands the student's preparation journey, identifies strengths and weaknesses, recommends the next best action, and keeps the student motivated until the day of the GATE examination.

---

## Long-Term Vision

To become the most trusted AI mentor for every GATE aspirant.
